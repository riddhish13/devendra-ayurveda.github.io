Cautions

Asthma can be serious: these don't replace an inhaler or other medicine your doctor has prescribed. Keep using them, and get urgent help for a severe attack. Both contain Somlata (ephedra), so ask your doctor before taking them together, and first of all if you have heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid disease or glaucoma, or take an MAOI antidepressant. Asthama contains purified borax: never exceed the dose.
